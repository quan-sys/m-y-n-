9-1C-1 INPUTS. Read `data/price_history/2026-07-24/deadjusted_close.csv.gz` (columns `ticker, date, raw_close, cumulative_factor, adjustment_confidence`; raw_close is in THOUSAND_VND per share) and `data/share_count/2026-07-22/share_count_point_in_time.csv` (columns `ticker, quarter, measurement_date, source_fiscal_year, available_from, shares_issued_derived, staleness_days, status`; status in {PIT_ISSUED_OK, PIT_TREASURY_PRESENT, NO_AVAILABLE_ANNUAL}). Do NOT modify either input.

9-1C-2 PRICE-DATE ALIGNMENT. `measurement_date` is a CALENDAR quarter-end and may fall on a non-trading day. For each (ticker, quarter), the price used is `raw_close` on `price_date_used` = the LATEST trading date in the de-adjusted series for that ticker with `date <= measurement_date`. If the ticker has NO price row on or before `measurement_date` (e.g. not yet listed), set market_cap NULL and market_cap_status = NO_PRICE. Do NOT interpolate, forward-fill from after the date, or fabricate a price.

9-1C-3 MARKET-CAP FORMULA. market_cap_thousand_vnd = raw_close(price_date_used) * shares_issued_derived. UNITS: raw_close is THOUSAND_VND per share and shares_issued_derived is a share count, so the product is in THOUSAND_VND. Do NOT multiply or divide by 1000 anywhere. (To express in VND, a downstream consumer multiplies by 1000; do NOT do that here.)

9-1C-4 PRICE CONFIDENCE IS PER-ROW, NOT PER-TICKER. `price_confidence` for a (ticker, quarter) row equals the `adjustment_confidence` (OK or LOW) of the SPECIFIC price row at `price_date_used`. It must NOT be set to LOW merely because the ticker has any LOW date elsewhere in its history. State this explicitly.

9-1C-5 SHARE STATUS DETERMINES market_cap_status. Pass `status` through as `share_status`, and set market_cap_status by this exact mapping: PIT_ISSUED_OK -> OK; PIT_TREASURY_PRESENT -> UPPER_BOUND (shares_issued_derived includes treasury shares, so the resulting market cap is an UPPER BOUND on the true outstanding-share market cap); NO_AVAILABLE_ANNUAL -> NO_SHARE_COUNT with market_cap NULL. The NO_PRICE case from 9-1C-2 overrides to NO_PRICE. State that `price_confidence` (a price-quality flag) and `market_cap_status` (a share-quality flag) are INDEPENDENT and both are carried; do NOT collapse them into one column.

9-1C-6 NO SHARE-COUNT ROW. If a (ticker, quarter) exists in the price universe window but has no row in the share-count table, do NOT invent one; only rows present in `share_count_point_in_time.csv` produce output rows (that file is the row spine, one row per its 12,096 ticker-quarters). State that the share-count table is the authoritative row set.
